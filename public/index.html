How your CID is used

This project uses your VATSIM CID and your historical ATC records to calculate quarterly totals, and for nothing else. It never asks for your password.

There is no login and no account database. The server does not store usernames, passwords, or cookies.

It collects only what the calculation needs. It does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Scope and accuracy

Results are estimates and may be incomplete or inaccurate. They are not an official VATSIM compliance decision.

The tool currently covers Division totals and a first pass at VATUSA ARTCC mapping. It does not check local rosters, visiting-controller rules, rating rules, or activity exceptions.

For a final decision, check the current official VATSIM, Division, and ARTCC guidance.
